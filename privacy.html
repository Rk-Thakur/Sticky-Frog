Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026  ·  Sticky Frog Signs & Shirts  ·  Birmingham, AL

1. Introduction

Sticky Frog Signs & Shirts ("Sticky Frog," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. Ever.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

When you contact us, request a quote, or place an order, we may collect:

  • Your name and contact information (email, phone number)
  • Shipping and billing address
  • Order details and artwork files
  • Payment information (processed securely through our payment provider — we do not store card numbers)
  • Any messages or notes you include with your order or inquiry

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, standard web analytics tools may collect:

  • Your IP address and browser type
  • Pages visited and time spent on our site
  • Referring website or search terms

This data is aggregated and anonymous — we cannot identify individual visitors from it.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Process and fulfill your order
  • Communicate with you about your quote, proof, or order status
  • Send invoices and receipts
  • Respond to your questions or support requests
  • Improve our products, services, and website experience
  • Send occasional updates or promotions (you may opt out at any time)

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your data. We may share limited information only with trusted third parties who help us operate our business:

  • Shipping carriers (UPS, FedEx, USPS) to deliver your order
  • Payment processors to handle transactions securely
  • Email service providers to send order confirmations and updates
  • Law enforcement or legal authorities if required by law

All third-party service providers are contractually required to keep your information secure and use it only for the purpose we specify.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill your order, comply with legal obligations, and resolve any disputes. Order records are typically kept for 3–5 years for accounting and warranty purposes. You may request deletion of your data at any time (subject to legal retention requirements).

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve your browsing experience. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect some website functionality. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • File a complaint with relevant data protection authorities

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the details below.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no data transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to contact us via phone for highly sensitive information.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our services after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
