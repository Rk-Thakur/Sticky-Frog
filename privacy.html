Introduction

About Us: Sticky Frog was founded in Troy, Alabama in 2007 by Alexis and Jennifer Hill. We offer screen printing, embroidery, signs, banners, vehicle graphics, DTF printing, and more — serving Troy, Luverne, and customers across the country.

Sticky Frog Signs & Shirts ("Sticky Frog," "we," "us," or "our") is a locally owned custom printing and signage business based in Troy, Alabama, serving customers across Alabama and nationwide. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. Ever.

This page outlines our Return Policy and how we handle your personal information. If you have any questions, please reach out to us using the contact details below.

Return Policy

All custom items are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged.

When Exceptions Apply

We will issue a replacement or full refund if your order:

  • Arrives damaged during shipping
  • Is defective due to a production or material fault
  • Contains an error made by Sticky Frog — such as incorrect colors, wrong item, or a misprint that differs from the approved proof

Proof Approval Process

Every order includes a digital proof review before production begins. The buyer is responsible for carefully reviewing and approving the proof — including design, spelling, placement, colors, and sizing. Any changes must be requested and approved prior to production. Once you have approved the proof, Sticky Frog is not liable for errors that were present and visible in that approved proof.

How to Report an Issue

If you believe your order qualifies for an exception, please contact us within 7 days of receiving your order. Include your order number and clear photos of the issue so we can resolve it as quickly as possible.
